Equine Companion

Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Introduction

Equine Companion (“we”, “us”, or “our”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Data We Collect

All horse records, health logs, reminders, and settings you create in Equine Companion are stored locally on your device only. We do not transmit your personal horse data to any server.

If you subscribe to a paid plan, RevenueCat (our payment processor) handles purchase verification. RevenueCat may collect anonymized purchase and device information as described in their privacy policy.

Location Data

If you enable the weather feature, your approximate GPS coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo to retrieve your local forecast. No account is required and Open-Meteo does not store or log your location data. You can disable weather at any time in Settings.

Location is also used when you record a GPS ride. Ride coordinates are stored locally on your device only and are never transmitted to any server.

Voice Input

If you use voice input, your device's built-in speech recognition processes audio in real time to convert it to text. On Android this uses Google's speech recognition service; on iOS it uses Apple's speech recognition. In both cases audio may be sent to that provider for transcription and is not stored by us. Voice input is optional — you can disable microphone access at any time in your device's app permissions.

Notifications

If you grant notification permission, Equine Companion schedules local reminders on your device. These notifications are processed entirely on-device and are not transmitted externally.

Analytics

We do not use third-party analytics SDKs. No usage data, crash reports, or behavioral analytics are collected in this version of the app.

Data Storage and Deletion

All app data is stored in your device's local storage. Deleting the app removes all locally stored data. We have no ability to access or restore your data once the app is deleted.

Children's Privacy

Equine Companion is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at taurstudios@gmail.com.
