Critter Companion

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

The short version

Critter Companion keeps your records on your phone. There’s no account, no server of ours, no ads, no analytics and no location. I can’t see your data, so I can’t lose it, sell it or hand it over.

Data you enter

Everything you create — pets of any species, health and vaccine records, preventives and medications, weights, daily litter box checks, food, feeding and care routines, tasks, journal entries and photos, documents, contacts, and expenses, and, with the Houseplants module, your plants with their care logs and photos — is stored only on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else.

Health records, contacts and money records are encrypted on your device. You can also put health, documents and money screens behind Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode with App Lock. Biometric checks are done by your phone; the app only learns whether they succeeded.

Camera and photos

The app asks for the camera or your photo library only when you add a photo or a document. Photos are copied into the app’s own storage on your phone.

Other people’s details

If you record vets, sitters or other contacts, you are keeping their details on your own device. Please only record what you need.

Files you choose to share

Backups and PDFs (sitter sheet, vet summary, lost pet flyer) are made on your phone and handed to your share sheet. Where they go from there is up to you. A backup can be protected with a password.

Location

Critter Companion never asks for your location and has no feature that uses it.

Voice input

If you use voice input, your device’s built-in speech recognition turns what you say into text, on-device where your phone supports it. Otherwise, on Android this uses Google’s speech recognition service and on iOS it uses Apple’s, and audio may be sent to that provider for transcription. It is never stored or collected by us. Voice input is optional — you can turn off microphone access at any time in your device’s app permissions.

Notifications

Reminders (medications, vaccines, preventives, document expiry dates, tasks, care routines, food running low, the optional daily litter box check and, with Houseplants, watering and plant care) are scheduled locally on your device and are not transmitted anywhere.

Purchases

Modules and bundles are optional one-time purchases through the App Store or Google Play. RevenueCat verifies them using an anonymous identifier, as described in their privacy policy. RevenueCat receives your purchase history only. Your records are never part of that, and checking purchases is the only time the app goes online.

Analytics and tracking

None. No analytics SDKs, no crash reporting that phones home, no advertising identifiers, no tracking.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes everything it stored. See Data Deletion for details.

Children’s privacy

Critter Companion is not directed at children under 13, and it collects no personal information from anyone.
