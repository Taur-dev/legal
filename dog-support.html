How do I unlock Pro?

Open Settings and tap Unlock Pro, or tap any Pro feature. Pro is a single one-time purchase — no subscription.

How do I restore my purchase on a new phone?

Settings → Restore purchases, signed in to the same App Store or Google Play account you bought with.

How do I move my records to a new phone?

On the old phone, Settings → Backup → Create backup (add a password if you like, and include photos). Save the file somewhere you can reach from the new phone, then on the new phone choose Restore from backup.

I forgot to back up and deleted the app

I’m sorry — because nothing is stored on a server, there’s no copy to recover. Regular backups are the only safety net.

I found a bug

Please email with what you were doing, what you expected, your phone model and OS version, and the app version (Settings → About). The in-app Send feedback button fills most of that in for you.

I have a feature request

I’d love to hear it. I read every message.
