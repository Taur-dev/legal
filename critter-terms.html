Purchases

Critter Companion is free, with complete records for every pet of any species, the daily litter box check for cats, and the “Is this plant safe?” lookup. Optional modules add care knowledge for one kind of pet (Dog, Cat) or for houseplants. Modules marked “Coming soon” can’t be bought until they’re released.

Each module is a one-time, non-consumable purchase through the App Store or Google Play, subject to their terms. A bundle is a one-time purchase of credits that you spend on modules of your choice, now or when later modules are released. There is no subscription and nothing renews. You can restore purchases on your other devices, and Family Sharing is supported where the store offers it.

No Veterinary or Professional Advice

Critter Companion records and organizes information; it does not give veterinary or professional advice. Daily check cards, due dates, reminders and care templates are general aids — always follow your veterinarian, and call them straight away in an emergency.

Plant toxicity answers and knowledge base articles are general information drawn from the sources they cite (such as the ASPCA, AAHA, AVMA and university veterinary schools), not veterinary advice. A plant listed as non-toxic can still upset a pet’s stomach, and a plant that isn’t listed is unknown, not safe. If you think a pet has eaten something harmful, call your vet or a pet poison line.
