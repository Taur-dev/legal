How do I get Pro?

Tap anything marked Pro (a locked Stats section, Strike Two, Timeline or the Review Deck) to open Go Pro. Pro is a single one-time purchase — no subscription.

How do I restore my purchase on a new phone?

Open Go Pro and tap Restore purchases, signed in to the same App Store or Google Play account you bought with.

I have a promo code

Open Go Pro and tap Redeem a code. On iPhone the App Store’s own code sheet opens; on Android, type the code and Google Play takes it from there.

How do I move my stats to a new phone?

On the old phone, Settings → Your data → Back up now (add a password if you like). Save the file somewhere you can reach from the new phone, then on the new phone choose Settings → Your data → Choose a backup file. Restoring replaces what’s on the new phone.

I deleted the app without a backup

I’m sorry — because nothing is stored on a server, there’s no copy to recover. A backup now and then is the only safety net.

The Daily reminder isn’t arriving

Check it’s on in Settings → Daily reminder, and that notifications are allowed for Trivialium in your phone’s settings. It skips any day you’ve already played the Daily. Open the app now and then: it schedules a week ahead each time.

A question is wrong

Thank you for catching it. Use Send feedback in Settings and tell me the question and what’s wrong; I’ll fix it in the next update.

I found a bug

Please email with what you were doing, what you expected, your phone model and OS version, and the app version (Settings → About & credits). The in-app Send feedback button fills most of that in for you.

I have a feature request

I’d love to hear it. I read every message.
