Dog Companion

Data Deletion

Last updated: October 2026

Dog Companion stores all of your records on your device. There is no account and no server holding your data. Deleting the app permanently removes it.

How to delete your data

  1. Long-press the Dog Companion app icon
  2. Tap Uninstall (Android) or Remove App → Delete App (iOS)
  3. Confirm

This immediately and permanently removes everything the app stored, including encrypted records and their keys. There is no copy on our end, so it cannot be undone. Any backup, CSV or PDF files you saved elsewhere are yours to delete separately.

What is deleted

  • Dog profiles, titles and photos
  • Health, vaccine, preventive and medication records, weights and clearances
  • Documents and contacts
  • Food, feeding, grooming and activity records
  • Tasks, journal entries and the gallery
  • Expenses and income
  • Heats, progesterone, breedings, litters and puppies
  • Buyers, waitlists, placements and payments
  • Shows and show entries
  • Settings, including your kennel profile and App Lock choices

Location

If you turned on the walk-conditions card, approximate coordinates were sent to Open-Meteo to fetch a forecast. Nothing is stored by us. Turn the card off in Settings, or revoke location in your device settings, at any time.

Pro purchase

The Pro unlock is handled by the App Store or Google Play, with verification through RevenueCat under an anonymous identifier, not your name or email. To ask for that purchase record to be deleted, email us — note it may stop Pro restoring on a new device.
