Frequently Asked

How do I unlock a module?

Open the More tab and tap Modules. Choose the animal you keep — Poultry, Goats, or Rabbits — and use the unlock button on its page. Each module is a one-time purchase; there are no subscriptions and nothing auto-renews.

How do I restore a purchase on a new device?

Open the app, go to More → Modules, and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed into the App Store or Google Play with the account you used to make the purchase.

Will my data move to a new phone?

Homestead Companion stores everything on your device. If you set up your new phone from a device or cloud backup (iCloud on iPhone, Google backup on Android), your app data moves with it. Your unlocked modules can always be restored on the new device using Restore Purchases (see above).

I found a bug. What should I do?

Please email homesteadcompanionapp@gmail.com with:

  • What you were doing when the issue happened
  • What you expected vs. what occurred
  • Your device model and OS version
  • The app version (More → About)
