Trivialium

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

The short version

Trivialium keeps everything on your phone. There’s no account, no server of ours, no ads and no analytics. I can’t see how you play, so I can’t lose it, sell it or hand it over.

What the app stores

Your answers (which question, whether you were right, how long you took), your games and Daily results, drill scores, your Review Deck and your settings are stored only on your device. They are not sent to us or to anyone else.

The questions themselves ship inside the app. Trivialium never downloads questions and never contacts the question sources while you play.

The only network call

The optional Pro unlock is a one-time purchase through the App Store or Google Play. RevenueCat checks it using an anonymous identifier, as described in their privacy policy. That is the only thing Trivialium sends over the internet, and your answers and stats are never part of it.

Files you choose to share

Backups and stats exports (CSV or JSON) are made on your phone and handed to your share sheet. Where they go from there is up to you. A backup can be protected with a password.

Notifications

The Daily reminder is off unless you turn it on. It’s scheduled locally on your device and is not sent through any server.

Analytics and tracking

None. No analytics SDKs, no crash reporting that phones home, no advertising identifiers, no tracking.

Deleting your data

Settings → Your data → Erase my data clears it, and deleting the app deletes everything it stored. See Data Deletion.

Children’s privacy

Trivialium is not directed at children under 13, and it collects no personal information from anyone.
